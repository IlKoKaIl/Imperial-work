## Error Types

- Concept: did not know what method or theorem applied.
- Algebra: knew the method but made a derivation/manipulation mistake.
- Technique: answer lacked words, definitions, procedure, or examiner-friendly structure.
- Timing: correct route but too slow.

## Repair Rules

- Concept errors: read the matching notes section and write a 5-line explanation from memory.
- Algebra errors: redo the derivation twice, once slowly and once exam-speed.
- Technique errors: rewrite the answer as a reusable template in `exam-procedures.md`.
- Timing errors: redo the same question with a strict 25-30 minute cap.
