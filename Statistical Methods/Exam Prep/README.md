# Statistical Methods Exam Prep Command Center

Exam: Wednesday 3 June 2026. Assumption: closed-book, 2 hours, 4 questions, all questions answered. This version starts properly on Monday 18 May, with Sunday 17 May kept as light setup.

This prep kit implements a past-paper-led rebuild. Because the course was taught a while ago, the first few days are solution-guided: use answers to relearn the method, then close them and reproduce the solution from memory. Do not passively reread both note PDFs cover to cover.

## How to Use This Folder

1. Today, open `start-here-may-17.md` and do the light setup.
2. From Monday 18 May, open `day-by-day.md` each morning and do that day's work.
3. For May 18-22, use `worked-solution-protocol.md` before expecting closed-book performance.
4. Start each session with either `guided-warmup-prompts.md` or `closed-book-prompts.md`, depending on the date.
5. Mark harshly only after you have made an honest reproduction attempt.
6. Repair using `topic-map.md` to jump to the relevant notes section.
7. Track paper scores in `score-tracker.csv` once timed papers begin.
8. Use `theory-answer-bank.md` for short mark-scheme-style theory/procedure answers.
9. Use the recall/procedure sheets on June 1-2 only after you have tried recall from memory.

## Daily 4 Hour Block: May 18-22 Solution-Guided Rebuild

| Block | Time | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Guided warm-up | 20 min | Read the relevant primer/recall sheet, then write what you remember. |
| Worked solution study | 70 min | Read one solution actively, line by line, annotating the method. |
| Closed reproduction | 45 min | Close the solution and reproduce the derivation/procedure from scratch. |
| Near-neighbour question | 65 min | Try a related paper part with limited peeking. |
| Log and repair | 40 min | Add errors to `error-log.md` and patch with targeted notes. |

Peeking is allowed during the first block, but the closed reproduction is the real learning.

## Daily 4 Hour Block: May 23 Onward Timed Practice

| Block | Time | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Closed-book recall | 20 min | Write formulas, definitions, and derivation skeletons from memory. |
| Attempt | 90 min | Do selected paper questions without answers. Use 30 min per full question. |
| Mark and log | 60 min | Compare to solutions, assign marks, write error-log entries. |
| Targeted repair | 50 min | Read only the relevant note pages and redo missing steps. |
| Reattempt | 20 min | Redo one failed derivation from scratch. |

If you have an extra 30 minutes, spend it on deeper marking and a second reattempt.

## Non-Negotiables

- During May 18-22, answer sheets are allowed for learning, but only through the worked-solution protocol.
- From May 23 onward, no answer sheet until an honest timed attempt exists.
- Every repeated mistake gets a named repair task.
- Every derivation must be reattempted without looking at the previous solution.
- If a day is missed, skip low-priority reading before skipping past-paper attempts.
- Keep the final sheets to one frequentist page, one Bayesian page, and one procedures page.

## Definition of Ready for the Exam

- A full paper can be finished in 2 hours.
- Common MLE, MoM, posterior, evidence, and Neyman/test procedures can be written closed-book.
- The error log has no repeated conceptual mistake after May 31.
- The June 2 session is light recall only: no new hard content.
