# Worked-Solution Protocol

Use this during May 18-22. The goal is to learn from solutions without just reading them and nodding.

## The Four Passes

## Pass 1: Question First, 3-5 Minutes

Read the question before the solution.

Write:

- Topic: distribution / MLE / test / posterior / evidence / hierarchical / SBI.
- Given objects: data, parameters, known constants.
- Expected output: estimator, posterior, interval, test, derivation, explanation.
- First formula you think might apply.

It is fine if this is mostly blank at the start.

## Pass 2: Active Solution Read, 15-25 Minutes

Read the answer sheet line by line.

For each step, annotate one of:

- Definition: a thing to memorise.
- Setup: likelihood, prior, test statistic, or integral.
- Algebra: manipulation you must be able to reproduce.
- Examiner phrase: wording that gets marks.

Do not copy the whole solution. Extract the method.

## Pass 3: Closed Reproduction, 15-30 Minutes

Close the answer sheet.

Reproduce the solution from scratch using only:

- The question.
- Your one-line method notes.
- The relevant recall/primer sheet if totally stuck.

Mark the reproduction, not the reading. Anything you could not reproduce goes into `error-log.md`.

## Pass 4: Near-Neighbour Question, 30-60 Minutes

Do a related question with limited peeking:

- Same topic, different year.
- Try unaided for 10-15 minutes.
- If stuck, peek only at the next structural step.
- Close the solution and finish the derivation yourself.

For frequentist topics, the Part 1 problem bank is ideal for near-neighbour practice:

- Poisson moments: Part 1 Problem 1.3.
- Maxwell-Boltzmann and estimator bias: Part 1 Problem 1.8.
- Significance and p-values: Part 1 Problem 1.9.
- Exponential lifetime MLE: Part 1 Problem 1.10.

## Peeking Rules

- Good peek: "What is the next setup equation?"
- Bad peek: reading the whole final answer and moving on.
- After every peek, close the answer and write the next three lines yourself.
- A question is not complete until you can explain the route without looking.

## When to Switch to Closed-Book

From May 23 onward, use solutions only after a timed or semi-timed attempt. By May 27, full papers should be closed-book, because the exam will punish recognition without recall.
