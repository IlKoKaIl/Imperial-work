## Wasserman CMU Selection

Public CMU page:

https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~larry/%3Dstat705/

Read these first:

- Lecture 1: probability basics and distributions.
- Lecture 13: estimators, MoM, MLE, Bayes estimators.
- Lecture 16: hypothesis testing.
- Lecture 18: confidence intervals and inverting tests.
- Lecture 23: linear regression and regularisation.
- Lecture 24: Bayesian inference and priors.
- Lecture 25: model selection, CV, AIC, BIC, Bayes.

Then add:

- Lecture 17: goodness-of-fit and permutation tests.
- Lecture 20: Monte Carlo and bootstrap.
- Lecture 26: nonparametric regression and bias-variance.
- Lecture 27: KL/TV/Hellinger distances if interview prep starts touching information theory or model misspecification.

## Quant Interview Books

The email mentions:

- Xinfeng Zhou, *A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews*.
- Mark Joshi, *Quant Job Interview Questions and Answers*.

These are useful as puzzle/question banks. Do not spend the first week reading them passively.

Suggested use:

- Pick 5 probability/statistics questions at a time.
- Attempt each for 10 minutes.
- Read solution only after writing your own attempt.
- Add missed patterns to the error log.

Use legal copies only: purchased copy, university library, or legitimately provided material. If you place legally available notes/PDFs in this folder, I can summarise and turn them into targeted drills.

## What Not To Do

- Do not read all 27 CMU PDFs before solving questions.
- Do not start with ESL chapters 1-10 as a first pass.
- Do not only grind LeetCode and neglect probability/statistics.
- Do not memorise puzzle answers without learning the underlying trick.
- Do not leave behavioural prep until the end.
