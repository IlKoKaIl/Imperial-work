V6+ PLAN (COMPACT) - MARCH TO SEPTEMBER 2026
Goal: consistent V6+ by September 30, 2026 while keeping physique work.

This can break your plateau if you do 3 things:
1) Keep session quality high (good rest, capped attempts).
2) Progress finger loads slowly every 1-2 weeks.
3) Deload every 4th week.

==================
1) WEEKLY SCHEDULE
==================
MON: Climb hard + Max hangs
TUE: Gym A (7 exercises x 2 working sets each)
WED: Climb volume + Repeaters
THU: Home core (8-10 min) + optional 20-30 min easy cardio
FRI: Climb project day (no full hangboard workout)
SAT: Gym B (7 exercises x 2 working sets each)
SUN: Full rest

Keep Thu + Sun as recovery focused.

THU HOME CORE:
- 3 rounds
  1) Hanging leg raises x 8-12
  2) Hollow body hold 20-35s
  Rest 45-60s between rounds
- Keep all sets 1-2 reps/seconds away from failure.

==============================
2) CLIMBING WARM-UP (ALL 3 DAYS)
==============================
1) 5-8 min general warm-up
2) External shoulder rotations: 2 sets
3) 6-8 easy boulders, gradually harder
4) Recruitment hangs only: 3 x 10s easy-moderate, full rest

Important:
- Do NOT do full repeaters before climbing.
- It may not feel very pumped, but it still adds finger fatigue.

===========================
3) HANGBOARD (EXACT PLAN)
===========================
A) MONDAY - MAX HANGS (AFTER HARD CLIMB)
- 20 mm edge, half crimp
- 6 sets x 7-10s
- Rest 2.5-3.5 min
- Intensity: RPE 8.5-9 (hard, never failure)
- Progression: when all 6 sets are strong, add 1-2 kg or reduce assistance slightly

B) WEDNESDAY - REPEATERS (AFTER VOLUME CLIMB)
- 20 mm edge, half crimp, feet assisted
- 7s on / 3s off x 6 reps = 1 set
- 5 sets total
- Rest 2-3 min between sets
- Intensity: set 4-5 hard but controlled
- Progression: reduce foot assistance slightly every 1-2 weeks

C) FRIDAY - NO FULL FINGERBOARD WORK
- Keep Friday for best project performance.
- Only do recruitment hangs from warm-up.
- If you insist on Friday fingerboard: do 2 sets repeaters max, and then skip Wednesday repeaters that week.

Safety rule:
- Finger pain >2/10 lasting >48h = remove max hangs + campus for 7 days.

=========================
4) CLIMB DAY STRUCTURE
=========================
MON (HARD DAY) - 85-95% effort
- 4-6 hard problems (board or gym set)
- Max 4 attempts per problem
- Rest 3-4 min between hard tries
- Total hard attempts: 16-24
- Then max hangs

WED (VOLUME DAY) - 65-80% effort
- 12-18 total problems
- Mix board and gym sets (example 6 + 6)
- 1-2 attempts per problem
- Rest 1.5-2.5 min
- Then repeaters

FRI (PROJECT DAY) - 90-100% effort
- 2-3 project problems (board preferred for MoonBoard goal)
- 4-6 attempts per project, then move on
- Rest 4-5 min between serious attempts
- Optional: finish with 2-3 easier quality sends

Stop rule (all climb days):
- Stop when power drops clearly for 3 attempts in a row.

==============================
5) GYM DAYS (YOUR PREFERENCES)
==============================
Format: 7 exercises x 2 working sets each.
Compounds: keep 1-2 reps in reserve.

TUE - GYM A (Lower + Push + Core)
1) Zercher squat
2) Deadlift or RDL
3) Chest press (flat or incline)
4) Overhead press
5) Triceps cable extension
6) TRX: 45s plank + 30s rest, 45s body saw + 30s rest, 45s mountain climber + 30s rest, repeat 3 rounds
7) Face pull or external rotation

SAT - GYM B (Pull + Arms + Forearm Rehab)
1) Weighted pull-ups
2) Chest-supported row
3) Cable bicep curls or hammer curls
4) Cable wrist extensions
5) Cable wrist curls
6) Lock-off isometrics (90-120 degrees, 5-8s)
7) Lateral raise or reverse fly

==========================
6) WEIGHTED PULL-UP PLACEMENT
==========================
Keep weighted pull-ups on Saturday gym day.
Do not move heavy weighted pull-ups to climb days.
Reason: preserves Monday/Friday board performance and lowers elbow/finger overload risk.

==========================
7) POWER / CAMPUS QUESTION
==========================
Yes, you need power, but first get it through hard/dynamic board climbing.
Campus is optional and only after May 2026 if pain free.

Campus starter (1x/week max):
- 4-6 total sets
- big rungs
- feet-on laddering or controlled double touches
- 2-3 min full rest
- no 1-3-5 maxing yet

====================
8) PROGRESSION BLOCK
====================
Mar 16 - Mar 29: reset fatigue and establish routine
Mar 30 - May 24: base build (high-quality volume + consistent repeaters)
May 25 - Jul 19: harder board intensity + max hang progression
Jul 20 - Sep 06: project conversion, slightly lower gym fatigue
Sep 07 - Sep 30: peak and send phase

Deload every 4th week:
- Climbing volume down 35-40%
- Max hangs: 3 sets only
- Repeaters: 3 sets only
- Gym loads down 10-15%

=====================
9) NUTRITION (COMPACT)
=====================
Protein: 130-150 g/day
Creatine: 5 g/day
Pre-climb carbs: 60-120 min before session
If cutting, keep deficit small to protect climbing performance

=========================
10) SIMPLE TRACKING
=========================
Every 4 weeks track:
1) 20 mm 7s hang load (added or assisted)
2) MoonBoard benchmark list (8-10 climbs): flash rate + sends within 3 tries
3) Best gym grade sent in <=5 attempts
4) Finger pain score and bodyweight trend

End of note.
